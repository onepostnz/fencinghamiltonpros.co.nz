Types Of Fencing

At Hamilton Fencing and Decks, we specialise in residential fencing. We offer a variety of different types to meet our clients’ needs. Our most popular type is wood fencing, which provides privacy while also adding an aesthetic appeal to the home. It comes in various styles such as picket fence, split rail, louver or lattice panels and vertical boards with top caps. For those looking for something more secure, chain-link fences may be the right choice. Its galvanized steel posts can hold heavy-duty fabric that will provide maximum security along with noise reduction benefits. Lastly, vinyl fencing is another great option as it requires minimal maintenance and offers superior durability compared to other kinds of materials used for fencing.

Each type has its own advantages and disadvantages depending on your specific requirements – from cost effectiveness to longevity to aesthetics – so you need to make sure you take all factors into consideration before making a decision about what kind of fence is best for your property.
